Meet the residents of Zisos

Every animal here has a story. Here are the stars of the park.

🐯
Big Cats

Bengal Tiger

The royal Bengal tiger is the pride of Zisos. Our pair roam a spacious, forested enclosure complete with a natural pool.

🦁
Big Cats

Asiatic Lion

See the majestic Asiatic lion, a rare species now found only in a handful of protected parks across the world.

🐘
Herbivores

Indian Elephant

Gentle giants that love their daily bath. Watch our mahouts care for them in the open elephant yard.

🦚
Birds

White Peacock

A dazzling display of plumage. Our white peacocks are a visitor favourite during the morning hours.

🦛
Aquatic

Hippopotamus

Spend time at the hippo pool and watch these massive animals surface and yawn.

Primates

Chimpanzee

Highly intelligent and playful, our chimpanzee troop is always full of activity.

Indian Rhinoceros

Emperor Penguin
