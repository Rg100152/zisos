Give an animal a better tomorrow

Adopting an animal at Zisos is a wonderful way to support its daily food, medical care and enrichment. Choose an animal, make a contribution, and we'll send you an adoption certificate and a photo of your new friend.

Prefer to simply donate? Every rupee goes directly towards the care of our rescued and resident animals.
