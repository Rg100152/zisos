About Zisos

A green haven for wildlife and people alike, right in the heart of the city.

Zisos Zoological Park is home to hundreds of animals across beautifully landscaped habitats. From the roar of the royal Bengal tiger to the playful antics of our chimpanzee troop, every visit is a new adventure.

Our mission is simple: connect people with nature, inspire a love for wildlife, and lead the way in conservation, rescue and animal welfare.
