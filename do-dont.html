Do's & Don'ts

Home  ›  Do's & Don'ts

Please Do

  • Keep the park clean and use the dustbins provided.
  • Follow the marked pathways and signage.
  • Keep children supervised at all times.
  • Carry drinking water and stay hydrated.
  • Respect the animals and give them space.
  • Photograph for personal use and enjoy your visit.

Please Don't

  • Do not feed the animals — it harms their health.
  • Do not tease, poke or throw objects at animals.
  • Do not use flash photography near the animals.
  • Do not litter or bring plastic bags into the park.
  • Do not play music or use loudspeakers.
  • Do not smoke or consume alcohol inside the park.

Entry to the park is subject to these rules. Thank you for helping us keep Zisos safe and beautiful.
