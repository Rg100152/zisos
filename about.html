A green legacy since 1921

Zisos Zoological Park was established in 1921 to celebrate a love for the natural world. What began as a modest collection of rescued animals has grown into one of the region's most-loved wildlife destinations, welcoming more than a million visitors every year.

The park was originally laid out on an old mango orchard. Even today, towering trees provide shade to our animals and guests, and the historic Baradari at its centre remains a proud landmark.

Our mission

We exist to inspire people to value and protect wildlife. Through world-class animal care, conservation breeding, education and rescue work, Zisos aims to be a beacon of hope for threatened species.
